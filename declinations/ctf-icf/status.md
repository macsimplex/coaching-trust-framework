## Status definition

*working-draft* — A community member is producing the declination from the federation's normative documents. The work is public and any feedback is welcome. The declination has **not** been reviewed or endorsed by the International Coaching Federation.

Platforms may implement a declination at *working-draft* status, provided they explicitly state this provisional status in their self-declaration. This is a contribution to the maturation of the declination, not a substitute for federation endorsement.

For the full status taxonomy of CTF declinations, see [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## Path to endorsement

The declination will be submitted to the International Coaching Federation for review once:

1. The full mapping of the ICF AI Coaching Framework and Standards V1.01 onto CTF's structure is complete.
2. The declination has been used by at least one platform implementation in good-faith working-draft mode, with feedback documented in [`../../validation/evidence-log.md`](../../validation/evidence-log.md).
3. The community pathway documented in [`../../certification-scheme/community-pathway.md`](../../certification-scheme/community-pathway.md) has been followed in full.
