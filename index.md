# Coaching Trust Framework

*An open framework for trustworthy AI coaching agents — published as a common good under CC BY-SA 4.0.*

---

## What CTF is

CTF is a shared, free, open framework that any platform can adopt, that any professional federation can adapt, and that any practitioner can read and verify. It articulates how an AI coaching agent should be built, tested, deployed and supervised so that the people who consult it can trust what is being offered to them.

CTF is **inductive**: a deliberately minimal common core, declined per professional federation (CTF-ICF, CTF-EMCC, ...), with the common core growing over time only by absorbing what has been observed to be common across multiple declinations.

CTF is **examinable**: empirical evidence is a structural object, helper agents are sollicited and inspectable, supervision is tripartite by design.

CTF is a **common good**: the founding principle, inscribed at the head of every key document, states that the common good prevails over any organization, including any organization that might one day emerge to steward this standard.

## Where to start, depending on who you are

- **You are curious and want a plain-language overview** → read [the EXPLAINER](./EXPLAINER) (English) or [l'EXPLAINER en français](./EXPLAINER.fr).
- **You are a coach evaluating AI coaching tools** → read [the EXPLAINER](./EXPLAINER), then ask the platforms you consider whether they self-declare conformance to CTF.
- **You are a platform building an AI coaching agent** → read [the README](./README), then [`certification-scheme/self-declaration`](./certification-scheme/self-declaration), then start with [CTF Designer](./tools/ctf-designer/).
- **You represent a professional federation** → read [the EXPLAINER](./EXPLAINER), then [`certification-scheme/federation-pathway`](./certification-scheme/federation-pathway).
- **You want to contribute** → read [`CONTRIBUTING`](./CONTRIBUTING), then [`GOVERNANCE`](./GOVERNANCE).
- **You are a researcher interested in evidence-based AI ethics** → read [`validation/methodology`](./validation/methodology) and [`validation/evidence-log`](./validation/evidence-log).
